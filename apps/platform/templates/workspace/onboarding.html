03 · REVIEW AND INSTALL

Give AMPVE its own space.

The original bootloader, partition table, factory app and stock XiaoZhi slot are preserved during installation. AMPVE's runtime will update Wi-Fi and its own settings in shared NVS after boot.

Download candidate review bundle — not installation approval

Exact write regions

    AMPVE writes and reads back the app first, then changes one boot-selection sector. Automatic rollback on the original bootloader is not assumed. USB recovery remains essential.

    Installation requires a publisher-signed release reviewed for your exact stock bootloader and partition table. A backup or a compiled candidate alone cannot enable it. No full-chip erase, eFuse changes, bootloader replacement or C6 flashing is included.

    04 · CONNECT AND MAKE IT YOURS

    Bring your board into AMPVE.

    After a successful startup, open Wi-Fi on the board. Join its temporary protected AMPVE network using the password shown on its screen and supply your Wi-Fi locally. Then tap Pair with my AMPVE account and enter its temporary code here.

    Stock firmware and upstream XiaoZhi codes do not work here. Pairing is separate from USB installation. The device appears online only after an authenticated heartbeat; board voice and Wi-Fi firmware updates remain separate milestones.
