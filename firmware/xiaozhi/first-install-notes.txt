AMPVE 0.1.10-improv-dev — first-install development candidate

Includes the local AMPVE shell, protected Wi-Fi setup and physically authorized Improv Serial, pairing, management heartbeat/settings, bounded startup recovery, and native signed OTA candidate code.

Targets Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B, P4 revision 1.3 with 32 MiB flash and the reviewed original partition layout. Initial installation proposes the empty ota_1 application slot plus one locally generated boot-selection sector. The stock bootloader, partition table, factory image and ota_0 are preserved during installation.

Physical startup, display/touch, C6 Wi-Fi, recovery and OTA are not yet validated. The XiaoZhi audio adapter, board Companion voice and microphone capture are not delivered by this first installation. Camera is outside this milestone.

This unsigned review candidate does not authorize installation. Exact stock bootloader/C6 compatibility, matching current private backups, separate storage, the generated recovery plan and owner approval of the exact writes remain required. Do not manually flash the review archive or use full-chip erase. Automatic rollback on the stock bootloader is unverified.
